Ronin engine, rigged build

The game engine at 480×270, top-down, now with our own ronin in place of the placeholder: a posable rig drawn pixel by pixel, so every move has real body motion (wind-up, lean, lunge, follow-through). He breathes calmly when idle, keeps the katana sheathed at his hip, draws it only to attack and resheathes after. Hit the training dummy to feel the impact.
