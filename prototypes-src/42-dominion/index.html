The ronin's dominion

The whole world from seed 12345, with every region lord playing by the rules he plays by: they build, grow their towns, tax, raise hosts and go to war. He holds four plots on a hillside near where he started. Pick a building and click his land to place it, hire hands, watch the homestead grow a tier, raise a few men, then run the years and watch the realms change.
