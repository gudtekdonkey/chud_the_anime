Prototype 34 · Companions and kit

Companions who fight beside him, dressed from one wardrobe

Companions are samurai built on his rig. They wear the same clothing, carry the same weapons and move with the personality traits, so every item fits everyone. Up to 30 follow him. Press Tab to open the kit screen and dress or arm anyone in the party.

WASDmove Jcut (again for the answer) Kpaired execution Erecruit · hold to lift Ghold here / with me Tabkit screen

In the kit screen: Q / E change who, W / S pick a slot, J choose, K take off, Space moves a companion between the party and camp. Everything can be clicked. Try giving Kuro the katana, or putting the kabuto on someone wearing a mask. Fill the party to 30 adds made-up companions to see the whole warband.

The companions

Kuro · the old spear

Yari · holds the line

Plants himself between you and them and keeps them at spear length. Moves grim and drilled (Grim, Soldier). Jingasa, chain shirt, hakama, tasuki, shin guards. Starts with Iron Oath.

Suzume · the sparrow

Twin tanto · flanker

Goes round the far side of whoever you are fighting and cuts from behind. Moves light and fidgety (Nimble, Restless). Ponytail, shinobi wrap, short kimono, long scarf. Starts with Split Tsuba.

Tetsu · the wall

Nodachi · breaker

Wades into the thickest knot of them for the wide, heavy cut. Moves heavy and slow (Heavy, Lumbering). Pilgrim's kasa, wild hair, monk's robe, prayer beads, iron kote. Waits at camp to start.

Allies read at a glance without any colour on their clothes: the ronin has cyan eyes, companions white, samurai red. Bodies stay near-black and clothing stays shades of black.

New this round

Up to 30

The warband falls in behind him in ranks of six. In a fight they spread out: an enemy with four on him already is left to the others. The HUD shows one small bar per companion, ten to a row. Everyone after the first three is made up from the same wardrobe, weapons and traits.

Down, and maybe dead

A companion cut to nothing kneels, with a bar draining under them for 15 s. Hold E beside them to lift them. If the bar runs out, or they are struck again while down, they die for good and their gear goes back in the bag.

Paired execution

Now and then: every paired execution says what a partner needs (a weapon, an item they wear, or a skill they know), and any companion nearby who has it can join. The crossing cut needs a blade drawn from a scabbard, so a spear can't join it. K shows who will join you. He glitches in on the near side as they close on the far side, you cross through the samurai together, hold, resheathe as one, and he falls on the click.

They level up, their own way

Everyone earns EXP and levels on the same curve as him (kills, and fights they stand near). You spend the ronin's points yourself in the kit screen. A companion spends their own, by weighted chance from their weapon and personality: a heavy spearman leans to vigor, a restless flanker to speed. Two companions alike on day one grow apart. Stats: vigor, edge, speed, focus.

Three ways to recruit

Met on the road: a wanderer stands bottom left. Freed from the enemy: every other wave marches a captive in; cut the guards down and free him. Hired at camp: the notice board, 30 mon (kills pay 5).

How it works

One kit for everyone

The hero and each companion carry the same kit: a weapon, one piece per wardrobe layer (11 layers) and charms. The ronin has 4 charm slots, a companion 2. Mon, shards, EXP and the four quick slots stay shared on INV.

Every piece is one object

Giving someone a piece takes it off whoever had it. Weapons always trade, so nobody is left unarmed. Hat and mask fit rules apply per person: a clash takes off whichever was there first and says why.

The weapon sets the role

A companion fights the way their weapon asks: katana duelist, yari holds the line, tanto flanks, nodachi breaks. Weapon poses and weights come straight from the weapon system, so a swap changes their moves at once.

Party of three

You plus up to 30 companions. Anyone you send to camp waits there. A companion seat is the same data a co-op player will use later.

In the code

Built on the open branches rather than beside them: the wardrobe's rig, cloth and 20 pieces (Clothing), the weapon art hooks and per-weapon poses (Weapon system), trait bakes (Personality traits), the red-grey samurai (Real enemies) and the HUD kit (Items HUD). INV grows one field; everything per person moves to a kit.
