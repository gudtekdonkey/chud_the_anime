Design session · the wardrobe

Dress the ronin

Back to the original compact design. Every piece of clothing is now an item he can wear or take off: the mantle, the scarf, the cape and the obi sash. All of them are shades of black, told apart by tone and by how they move. Tick the boxes to dress him, and all four directions update at once.

Size at game scale

At 2×, some outfits side by side, each facing down, up, right and left: bare, mantle only, mantle and cape, scarf and sash, and everything. The dashed line marks 26 px, his height.

The idle, built from subtle moves

  • Breath: the body sinks one pixel and rises.
  • Cloth: the cape hangs from the shoulders and sways in a light breeze, and the scarf and sash tails flutter.
  • Mantle flutter: the ragged edge swaps between two hand-drawn states.
  • Blink: both eyes go dark for a moment every few seconds.
  • Static: a cyan spark crackles on the hilt now and then, a hint of the glitch powers.
