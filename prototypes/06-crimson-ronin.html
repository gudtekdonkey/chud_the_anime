The wanderer, crimson ronin

Crimson cape, slimmed down to a ronin's build: narrow waist, a red obi sash, wide hakama trousers and wrapped shins. The cape and both sash tails are simulated cloth. They hang, lag behind when he moves, snap out on a dash and settle again on their own.

What changed

  • Slimmer: the torso is 4 pixels wide, down from 8, with the arms drawn apart from it and a sash at the waist.
  • Ronin clothes: wide hakama that splits between the legs, wrapped shins, sandals and a sash with two tails.
  • Cloth: the cape is a 7×9 grid of points and each tail is a chain. Where the cloth bunches up it darkens into folds, and the lit edge takes a bright crimson highlight.
  • Colours: 9 plus the blade: cloak light and shade, face, visor, wraps, and four crimsons for the cape's highlight, light, shade and fold.
