Items · pickups, consumables, relics

What the road leaves behind

Everything the ronin finds and uses, except clothing, which is on its own page. There are four kinds. Big items get the lock-on brackets and a prompt when he is close enough. Small pickups fly to him when he walks near. Consumables sit in the quick slots and each has a short use animation. Relics go in the charm slots and change how his skills behave. Every scene runs at game scale, and every icon is 16×16.

The HUD at 480×270. Top left: health (white) and Qi (cyan, notched in thirds). Top right: mon and glitch shards. Bottom: the weapon slot, four quick slots (keys 1 to 4, with counts) and four charm slots. On the way he picks up Qi and coins without stopping. The sealed chest is out of range, so it gets no brackets. He locks on to the fallen katana, takes it, and the weapon slot swaps. Then the shrine ahead locks on.

01

Big items

The only things that get the lock-on brackets. They snap in when he is in range and breathe by a pixel. A prompt names the verb, and pressing it plays the interaction.

02

Small pickups

No brackets and no button. They sit and idle until he comes within about two body-widths, then they fly to him and pop.

03

Consumables

Picked up like small items, and used from a quick slot. Each use is under a second, so it never breaks the flow of a fight.

04

Relics

Passive charms. They hover a pixel off the floor, and walking into one sends it straight to an empty charm slot. Each one bends one of his skills.
