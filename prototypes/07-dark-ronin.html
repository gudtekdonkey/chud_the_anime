The ronin, in the dark

Redrawn in the style of your references: a dark blue-grey silhouette with cyan eyes, a crimson cape and sash, and a faint reflection on a flat grey floor. Every other colour comes from his effects: embers off the cape, a cyan energy slash, a blink dash that breaks him into sparks and rebuilds him at the far end, and a crimson shard spell.

What changed

  • Silhouette: the body, hood and trousers are a dark blue-grey, with one lighter panel tone so the arms and legs still read. The face is just two cyan eyes.
  • Two accent colours, as in the robot sheet: cyan for the eyes, blade and blink, crimson for the cloth, embers and spell. The floor carries a mirror reflection.
  • Blink: he flashes, bursts into sparks that fly the way he's going, vanishes, and rebuilds from a ring of sparks at the far end. The cape arrives with him and settles.
  • Cast: sparks gather to his hand, then eight crimson shards fire outward with a ring flash.
