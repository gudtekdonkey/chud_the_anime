The turn

A lap round the floor. Before, facing west was a flip: the whole figure swapped in one frame. Now he turns through the facings between, 45° every 0.03 s (half a turn in about a tenth of a second), by the camera side, so the hilt and scabbard never jump. The cloth swings round on its own. Afterimages keep the facing he had when they were left.

Turn speed

The same run five times over, each turning at a different speed. He runs right, stops, runs back left, stops, then toward the camera, away, and along the diagonals. How long each in-between facing shows decides how quickly he comes round: with no turn he pops straight to the new side; slower, you see him swing round by the camera.
