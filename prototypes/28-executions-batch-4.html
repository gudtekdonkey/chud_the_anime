Eight stranger ways to die

Executions 28 to 35, after Fault Line: deaths built on one strange idea each, not a better sword stroke. A shadow that moves when he does not, a line of echoes that go off one by one, a cage of static, a seam in the world itself, a thread, a floor that throws, a sound that shatters, and a man deleted a line at a time. Same rig, same samurai, same pieces; every one still ends with him on the floor, or gone.
