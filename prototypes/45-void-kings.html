Void Kings

The voids are where the wars ended. What lives there now is what the wars left: the dead who were never buried or mourned, grown together into things far bigger than men. They are spirits, ghouls and abominations, and each race is one kind of war dead. Each is shown at night, when they walk, in the game's own pixels at its own scale. The small dark figure in the hat is the ronin.

I · mountains

The Hollow King

new · replaces the great toad

A warlord who lost everything on one field and would not lie down. It is a robe with nothing in it, as tall as a watchtower, and the arrows that killed it are still in it. A crown of broken spikes floats above the empty hood, and two cold points of light sit where the eyes were.

Moves
It never walks. It is simply nearer each time you look. The hem trails into ribbons that stream upward, never down.
Weapon
A blade of pale light as long as it is tall, planted point-down like a staff.
Scare
The crown tilts toward you and the eyes open wider. Nothing else moves. You run.
Knockout
The blade lifts once. Cut to white.
War
A lost battle on an open field.

II · forest

The Veiled Weaver

was the earth spider

The widows of a forest war, who went into the trees to look for their husbands. It is a shroud hanging in the canopy from threads that go up out of sight, with a white mask looking out. Eight legs of light come down from it, each longer than a tree. The soldiers it found hang under it, wrapped in thread.

Moves
One leg at a time, slowly. The body does not bob; it is carried, like a lantern on a pole.
Sign
Threads across the path at head height, lit faintly from inside.
Scare
A leg comes down in front of you, then another behind. The mask turns to face you.
Knockout
The legs close round you like fingers.
longer than a village
III · marsh, coast

The Drowned Serpent

was the river serpent

An army that was driven into a flooded river and never came out. It swims through the air as if the marsh were still underwater. Its body is clear enough to see its ribs, and the drowned are still inside it, carried along. Its head is a skull with antlers and a hanging jaw.

Moves
A slow wave from head to tail that never stops. It is always longer than the screen.
Sign
Reeds lying flat in a path wider than a road, still moving.
Scare
The head rises out of the dark right beside you and passes on without looking. The body keeps coming for a long time.
Knockout
The coils pass over you. Cut to black water.

IV · bamboo, plains

The Lantern Beast

was the nue

A burned town's dead and the war horses that burned with it, stitched into one thing. It has an iron war mask for a face and a horse's mane gone white. Its striped body stands high on four thin legs, and a serpent for a tail watches behind it. The funeral lanterns nobody lit for them hang from its spine, lit now.

Moves
Walks like a heron, lifting each leg high. The lanterns sway after every step.
Sign
Lantern light between the bamboo, far too high up.
Scare
Every lantern turns to you at once, and so does the tail.
Knockout
The mask comes down to your height. Its mouth is much bigger than the mask.
≈ 9 × the ronin, above ground
V · hills

The Thousand-Step

was the great centipede

A column of soldiers buried alive in a hill fort's tunnels, grown into one body. It rises out of the hillside like a tower, and each segment is one of them: a skull, and a pair of spears for legs. At the top, a war mask with mandibles and a great V-shaped helmet crest looks down. No one has seen how much of it is still underground.

Moves
Sways as a whole, slow, while the legs ripple fast in a wave from the ground up.
Sign
Hills with holes in them big enough for a house.
Scare
The ground opens beside you and the tower rises until the head is out of sight.
Knockout
The mask comes straight down.

How they're drawn. Each body is one layer of pale light: its brightness comes from shape and folds, and it thins out in dithered steps, 1 px at a time with no blur, where it fades. Nothing about them is solid: the edges crawl slowly, and the only fully opaque pixels are eyes, masks and crowns.

In the game. The five races take over the five creature slots in the travel code, and each keeps to its own ground. The great toad is dropped, and the Hollow King takes the mountains.

What they do to him (your rulings). Before level 11 they scare him off, then knock him out. He wakes in town to someone telling him to train before he goes out like that again: things aren't like they used to be. From level 11 a fight with one can kill him, and his heir goes on. At level 11 he can also claim his first plot, anywhere in the wilderness, and the voids can be pushed back.
