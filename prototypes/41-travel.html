      How it works

      What happens on the road, and how often

      Each zone he crosses (about 25 real seconds on foot) may bring one encounter: about one zone in 15 on a safe road, one in 8 in outlaw land, never two within three zones. The chance and the mix come from where and when he is; the bar under the road shows them live for the zone he is in.

      While he is away (onDay)
      • Road danger per region drifts toward its people's base (outlaw coasts worst, temple lands safest), pushed up by its camps, bands out and famine.
      • Outlaw bands: two to five real men of a camp walk the roads for days, then go home. A man killed on the road is gone from his camp.
      • Weather per region turns every one to four days, by season and ground.
      • Glitch storms: about eighteen a year (autumn most), each lives 8 to 20 days and wanders 1 to 3.5 zones a day. Where they pass, shrines go dark for days, a settlement's people glitch, a strong storm steals hours from a village, and strange things are left lying in the wild.
      Inside a storm (for the game)
      • People flicker, slice and double: about 1.6 × strength times a second.
      • His glitch powers run stronger (up to ×1.6: K's range, the slivers, Qi from hits) and, past the middle of a storm, wild: a glitch skill can misfire somewhere near.
      • Glitch shards in the road; residue left in the wild where it was strongest.
      • Lost time: past the middle, a zone can take hours from him.
