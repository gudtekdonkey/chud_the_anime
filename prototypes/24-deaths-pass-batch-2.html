Ten more assassinations

Deaths pass. The same batch 2 executions, with one change to how the enemy's body works, applied to every kill at once. His joints chase their poses on springs, so the hips lead, the chest follows and the arms, blade and head trail and overshoot, and as he dies they go loose. A body going down speeds up into the floor and stops dead on it; the rest of him flops on, dust comes up, a twitch or two, and the red eye goes out. Sword hits knock him away from the blade, he shakes through the hit pause, light and blood leave out the far side, and a killing blow gets two impact frames. Untick Death pass to see the batch as it was on the same loop.

Batch 2, built on the approved batch 1: the same ronin, the same samurai, the same pieces cut from the enemy's own pixels. Every enemy reacts in one continuous, eased performance that fits where and when he is hit: a flinch when the ronin appears, a guard coming up, a hand to the wound, a knee going. He only comes apart once he is already falling, a body cut in two keeps its legs, which fold under on their own, and everything ends lying on the ground. As before, the kill lands on the click.
