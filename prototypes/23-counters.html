Counters · one for every attack

Read the attack, answer it

Tap F as the blow comes. The enemies have fourteen different attacks, each with its own wind-up you can learn to read, and a glint that runs up the blade as the tell. If the blow lands within the counter window after your press, the counter that plays is the answer to that attack: the way a swordsman would really meet it. Press too early and it is only a block. Every card is one attack and its counter; the four cards at the end show ways to show the window.

The counter window

Tap F: a blow that lands within 0.2 s of the press is countered; a press earlier than that is only a block, which pushes him back and gives no counter. Four ways to show the window. Each loops a press in time, then a press too early. Recommended: A, the glint, as the default, since it adds no interface and the tell is already there, with B, the closing ring, as an assist option for players who want it spelled out.

Your call

  2. The window indicator: A with B as an assist option, or another?
  3. The window's length: 0.2 s to start. It could widen with power, like Glitch Dodge.
