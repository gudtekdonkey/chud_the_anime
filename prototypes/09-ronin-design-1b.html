Slimmer, two eyes, black mantle

Redrawn from your notes. The body and hips are much thinner, with the arms drawn apart from the torso. The hat is narrower at the same height-to-width ratio. The mantle is a shade of black, and he has two eyes. The three designs differ in height and eye style so you can pick his size. A katana sheath trails behind the hip.

Size at game scale

At 2×, left to right: round 1's base, then designs 1, 2 and 3. The dashed line marks the Glitch Samurai's height, about 26 px.

The idle, built from subtle moves

  • Breath: the body sinks one pixel and rises.
  • Hat lag: the hat follows the body a beat late, which makes it feel like a separate object with weight.
  • Mantle flutter: the ragged edge swaps between two hand-drawn states.
  • Blink: both eyes go dark for a moment every few seconds.
  • Static: a cyan spark crackles on the hilt now and then, a hint of the glitch powers.

Your call

  1. Size: design 1, 2 or 3?
  2. Eyes: close dots, wide dots or slits? Any design can take any eye style.
  3. Mantle: does the black mantle read against the body, or should it be a touch lighter?
