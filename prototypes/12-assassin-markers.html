Who can he assassinate?

The rule: K only locks onto an enemy when no other enemy is within the isolation distance. Grouped enemies protect each other. Each scene has the ronin on the left, a lone enemy (assassinatable) up top, and a pair below. One of the pair patrols away and back, so you can watch the marker switch on the moment he is left alone. When two enemies are both valid, the nearer one is K's pick and gets the full marker; the other gets a faint one.

Your call

Pick one or two to combine. A strong pairing is usually one marker ON the target (to say "this one") and one piece of feedback about WHY the others are not valid, like idea 4's link lines.

The isolation distance: drag the slider to feel it. 36 px is about one and a half body heights.
