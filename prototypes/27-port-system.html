Rig v2 · the port system

Every move, every direction

The game's own animations, read straight from src/anims/poses.js, and personality idles and walks from src/traits/bake.js. None of them was redrawn. Each side-view pose goes through one function, port(), onto rig v2's skeleton, and is baked facing all eight ways in his default outfit, cloth and all. The old side-view rig sits in the middle of the compass for comparison.

What port() adds to a side pose

  1. The angles carry over. The near leg and arm become his right, the far ones his left, and the back-hand blade goes to his left hand.
  2. The hips follow the stride. The leading leg brings its hip round, so a lunge seen from the front turns.
  3. The chest turns into the blade. Whichever arm holds a sword, reaching forward turns that shoulder toward the target.
  4. The head stays on the target. It takes back most of the turn beneath it.
  5. Deep bends open the knees outward instead of folding them through his body.
  6. A hand on the hilt reaches for the real hilt at his left hip, from any side.
  7. Personality knobs carry over: a bowed head and dimmed eyes.
  8. Any move can override any rig v2 knob where the automatic turn is not what it needs. None of the moves below uses one.
