Mon and koku

The living world's economy on the simulation core. Every region keeps a market whose prices answer its own stock; land yields koku at the autumn harvest and taxes climb to the lords; caravans carry goods along the roads; every household earns by its trade and spends by its class. Play or fast-forward, pick a good to colour the map by its price, click a region to open its market and its lord.
