Design session · round 1 of 4 · silhouette

Our top-down ronin

One body, hand-drawn pixel by pixel from above at game scale: 24 wide and 26 tall, the same height as the samurai. A wide straw hat throws his face into shadow, and one cyan eye shows under the brim. A katana sits at his hip, and a black cloak ends in a ragged hem. Row 0 is the bare base, and options A to C only add the crimson cloth. Each one plays its idle facing down, up, left and right.

At game scale

The base and all three options, each in four directions, at 2×. A 1080p screen shows them at 4×. The hat, eye and crimson have to read here, not only zoomed in.

What I need from you

  1. The base first: does the body read right? Then pick A, B or C, or mix them, like "B's mantle with A's scarf tails".
  2. The hat: is it the right width? It can go flatter and wider, or taller and more pointed.
  3. The eye: one cyan eye, or two?
  4. The body: slimmer, or bulkier in the cloak?

Round 2 is the run in all four directions, round 3 the slash combo and slide, round 4 the glitch moves and death.
