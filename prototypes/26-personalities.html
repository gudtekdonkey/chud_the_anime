Personality · movement traits

Fifty-two ways to stand and walk

A personality is a short list of traits, each with a strength. A trait is only data: a few nudges to how he leans, breathes, holds his hands, strides and bounces, plus the small things he does when he stands still (the fidgets). Traits add together, so any two or three mix into a new character, and 0.5 of a trait is half as much of it. The same traits will fit the samurai, since they share his rig. With no traits he is exactly the ronin in the game today.
