Ronin engine, charged I test build

The rigged ronin build with a charged glitch double slash. Tap I for the normal double slash. Hold I to charge in its crouch (about 0.9 s to full), then release to fire the selected variant, scaled by how long you held. Keys 1–6 (or the buttons below the game) pick one of six variants. A field of training dummies shows the area of effect.

WASD / ←↑↓→ move J slash (press twice to combo) Space jump Shift / L ground slide K glitch teleport I tap: glitch double slash Hold I, release: charged variant 1–6 pick the variant U glitch sweep C sit and rest X die (to test)

The six charged variants

Each one keeps the double slash's body motion (crouch, vanish, lunge cut, second cut, freeze, resheathe) and lands its payoff on the sheath click. Distance and area grow with the charge: at full charge the dash covers about 110–170 px, walls and pillars permitting.
