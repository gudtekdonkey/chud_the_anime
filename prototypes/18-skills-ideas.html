Ten new ways to fight

Ten skills to sit beside the ones he already has: five actives, four passives and one ultimate. Each one loops below as a short scene against bare-headed samurai built on his own rig. Every action shows only its key frames: a short wind-up, a pose that leans into the motion, the hit with its flash and pause, the follow-through, and the resheathe. The enemies react to everything done to them, and when they die they fall with the momentum they had.
