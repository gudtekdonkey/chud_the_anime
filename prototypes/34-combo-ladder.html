Prototype 34 · J combo

The Combo Ladder

Every basic cut that lands is one point of basic skill. He starts with the two-cut J combo; at 40, 120, 250 and 450 landed cuts the chain grows by one, up to six. Cuts 3 to 6 are four different attacks: a spin cut, a front kick, a rising launch and a flash step. Each weapon does them its own way. Press J again in a cut's follow-through to flow into the next.

Flow: six basic cuts in a row

Each landed cut fills a pip. Six in a row, each within 1.6 s of the last, and Flow is ready.

Flow, the passive

  • Chain any six basic cuts: across several short combos is fine, as long as each lands within 1.6 s of the one before and no skill comes between them.
  • With Flow ready, the next skill that is still cooling down casts anyway: K, I (and Thousand Cuts), O, P, N or U. It then cools down again from the start.
  • A skill that is already ready casts normally and Flow stays for later. Flow waits 8 s.
  • Not the slide, and never an ultimate: Time Slice and anything else that needs a full Qi meter.
  • On the HUD: under the skill slots, J2 to J6 is his combo length now, and six pips count the chain. With Flow ready every slot still cooling down blinks cyan.
