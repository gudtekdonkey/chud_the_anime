Ten more ways to die

Executions 18 to 27, on the same rig, enemy and effects as batch 1. Each one starts the same way: he glitches out and lands beside the enemy. From there some are fast and heavy (a kick that throws the man into the air, a club blow that knocks him off his feet, lightning that lifts him off the floor), and some are quiet and precise (two fingers on the chest, a walk past, a reflection in the floor). The enemy reacts to everything in one continuous movement, and every death ends with him on the ground, whole or in pieces.
