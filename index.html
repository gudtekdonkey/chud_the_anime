Ronin engine, skills build

The game engine at 480×270, top-down, with our own rigged ronin: every move is a real pose (wind-up, lean, lunge, follow-through). He keeps the katana sheathed until he attacks, waits in guard after, and resheathes when calm. Now with charged skills: tap I for the glitch double slash or hold it for Thousand Cuts, hold O for the Crescent Moon, P for the Cross Rift, N for Mirror Meditation. Every hit feeds his Qi (bottom left); when it fills, Storm Chain wakes for 8 s and every hit chains lightning through the training dummies.
