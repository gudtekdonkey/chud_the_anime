Ronin engine, skills build

The game engine at 480×270, top-down, with our own rigged ronin: every move is a real pose (wind-up, lean, lunge, follow-through). He keeps the katana sheathed until he attacks, waits in guard after, and resheathes when calm. Now with charged skills: tap I for the glitch double slash or hold it for Thousand Cuts, hold O for the Crescent Moon, P for the Cross Rift, N for Mirror Meditation. Every hit feeds his Qi (bottom left); when it fills, Storm Chain wakes for 8 s and every hit chains lightning through the samurai. The room holds seven topknot samurai with health: hits make them flinch, heavy or quick hits stagger them, they turn to face him, and they die on their knees and fall. Clear the room and a new squad steps in. The skill bar at the bottom shows each skill's cooldown. K on a samurai standing alone (his bubble glows cyan and a K pops up over him) flashes to him and executes him.

Personality

How he stands, walks and runs comes from up to three personality traits, each with a strength (0.5 is half as much). There are 52 to pick from; prototype 26 shows them all side by side. Your mix is remembered in this browser.

Test a sprite strip

For testing new art: choose a sprite strip PNG (frames side by side in one row), pick which animation it is, and set the frame width. It replaces that placeholder at once. Nothing leaves your browser.
